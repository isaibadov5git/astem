# 06 — Brand & Design System

Visual style: **dark slate modern CleanTech** — subtle grid overlays,
glassmorphism cards, high-tech dynamic data badges.

## Color tokens

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `slate-navy` | `#0F172A` | Page background |
| `emerald` | `#00875A` | Primary accent, solid CTAs |
| `telemetry-cyan` | `#0284C7` | Glow, data highlights, charts |
| `light-grey` | `#F8FAFC` | Body text on dark |

Contrast ratios must meet WCAG AA on the dark ground.

## Typography

Crisp sans-serif: **Inter**, Montserrat, or SF Pro. Pick one and stick to it.

## Logo

[`assets/logo/astemlab-logo.jpeg`](../assets/logo/astemlab-logo.jpeg) —
bold, minimalist, geometric, with a thermal-gradient icon.

## Tone of voice

Enterprise, technical, evidence-led. Speak to engineers and CFOs, not consumers.
Lead with measured numbers ([04-metrics-and-feasibility.md](04-metrics-and-feasibility.md)),
never with hype.

## Motion

Framer Motion for scroll animations. Restrained: reveals and value counters, not
decorative movement.
