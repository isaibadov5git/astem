## Mission

Capture and upgrade industrial and urban waste heat through AI-governed
thermodynamic systems, to raise energy efficiency and cut carbon emissions.

## Core value proposition

Continuously wasted **low-grade heat (18 °C–40 °C)** — from industrial cooling
loops, refinery effluent, and urban transit / ventilation shafts — is converted
into **high-value, dispatchable thermal energy (60 °C–90 °C)**.

## The multiplier model (COP 3.8+)

```
1.0 kWh electrical input
+ 2.8 kWh recovered waste heat
────────────────────────────────
= 3.8 kWh usable thermal output
```

COP 3.8+ is the headline efficiency claim and appears across the site, the deck,
and the calculator. Keep it consistent everywhere.

## Target audience

- Industrial plant managers — oil & gas refineries, petrochemical facilities
- Municipal infrastructure authorities — district heating utilities, metro operators
- Enterprise ESG / sustainability officers
- CleanTech investors
