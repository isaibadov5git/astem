## Mission

Capture and upgrade industrial and urban waste heat through AI-governed
thermodynamic systems, to raise energy efficiency and cut carbon emissions.

## The problem — the industrial & urban heat paradox

Refineries and petrochemical plants discharge low-grade heat continuously through
cooling towers and process effluent. Metro tunnels add dense, high-frequency
passenger heat and stay 18 °C–30 °C year-round. **All of it is vented to the
atmosphere — while boilers a few hundred metres away burn natural gas to heat
feedwater.**

## Core value proposition

Continuously wasted **low-grade heat (18 °C–40 °C)** — from industrial cooling
loops, refinery effluent, and urban transit / ventilation shafts — is converted
into **high-value, dispatchable thermal energy (60 °C–90 °C)** at COP 3.8+.
