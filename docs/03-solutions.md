# 03 — Solutions by Sector

Two go-to-market tracks. On the website these are a tabbed / toggle interface.

## Track A — Downstream Oil & Gas and Industrial Facilities

**Applies to:** refineries, petrochemical plants, chemical industrial parks.

**Focus**
- Boiler feedwater pre-heating
- Industrial Cooling-as-a-Service (CaaS)
- Clustered manufacturing waste-heat nodes with shared off-take (industrial parks)

**Benefit**
- Direct natural gas consumption offset
- Reduced thermal load on cooling towers
- Scope 1 carbon mitigation

## Track B — Urban Transit & Municipal Infrastructure

**Applies to:** metro operators, district heating utilities, municipalities.

**Focus**
- Subway / metro ventilation shaft heat recovery
- Heat Purchase Agreements (HPAs) with municipal off-takers

**Benefit**
- Year-round tunnel cooling (comfort + equipment life)
- Low-cost municipal space heating and domestic hot water
