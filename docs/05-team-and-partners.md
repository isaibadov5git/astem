## Supporters & partners

### 🔴 Logos may NOT be published

The project lead confirmed **no written permission has been obtained from any of
the four institutions** ([`collab/DECISIONS.md`](../collab/DECISIONS.md)
decision 014).

**Do not put UFAZ, ASOIU, IDDA or Enterprise Azerbaijan logos on the website.**
Displaying a state agency's mark without consent is a real legal and reputational
risk, and it is exactly the kind of detail the audience for this site would
notice. The files stay in [`assets/partners/`](../assets/partners/) for internal
use only.

The **Supporters logo row planned in [07-website-spec.md](07-website-spec.md) is
therefore removed from the launch scope.**
