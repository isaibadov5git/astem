The website AstemLab  serves as the digital platform for AstemLab, a clean-tech venture based in Baku, Azerbaijan. The company focuses on capturing and upgrading industrial and urban waste heat through AI-governed thermodynamic systems to optimize energy efficiency and reduce carbon emissions.Core Value Proposition & MissionObjective: Transform continuously wasted low-grade heat (ranging from 18°C to 40°C) from industrial cooling loops, refinery effluent, and urban transit/ventilation shafts into high-value, dispatchable thermal energy (60°C to 90°C).Efficiency Model: Utilizes a COP 3.8+ multiplier engine where 1 kWh of electrical input combined with 2.8 kWh of recovered waste heat yields 3.8 kWh of usable thermal output.Technology & ArchitectureThe platform outlines a four-stage process powered by machine learning and process engineering:Thermal Capture: Sensors monitor low-grade waste heat across effluent streams, cooling infrastructure, and subway/metro ventilation shafts.AI Dynamic Optimization: Real-time machine learning algorithms adjust compressor speeds and fluid flow based on ambient conditions and live operational loads.Temperature Upgrade: Advanced water-to-water and air-to-water heat pump configurations elevate thermal energy efficiently.Industrial & Urban Delivery: Direct feed into refinery steam boilers, district heating networks, or domestic hot water systems.Solutions & Deployment SectorsRefineries & Petrochemicals: Pre-heating boiler feedwater to offset natural gas consumption and reduce thermal loads on cooling towers.Chemical Industrial Parks: Capturing clustered manufacturing waste-heat nodes for shared off-take.Metro & Transit Hubs: Reusing urban ventilation heat streams (targeting mapped urban potential across measured transit stations).Interactive Features & ToolsLive Impact Calculator: Allows plant operators and engineers to model potential annual energy savings, financial returns (gas cost offsets), and Scope 1 $\text{CO}_2$ emission reductions based on customizable input parameters (such as thermal capacity and operating hours).Modular Pilot Feasibility: Standardized 1.8 MW deployment modules engineered to deliver predictable annual thermal energy savings with a 4.9 to 7-year payback horizon.Team & Institutional BackingCore Team:
